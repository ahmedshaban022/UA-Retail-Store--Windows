# Shop hardware: acceptance checklist

Please lend the intended shop PC and the items below for the checks. Details are in the [acceptance plan](UAT-PLAN.md).

- The barcode scanner the till uses, with its USB cable: UAT-01, UAT-04, UAT-05.
- The 80 mm receipt printer and a roll of 80 mm receipt paper: UAT-04, UAT-08.
- An A4 printer and paper: UAT-08. Allow the tester to unplug the receipt printer's cable for this check.
- A second, clean Windows PC and a USB drive or external disk for the backup: UAT-16.
- Permission to stop the application while it is posting a sale: UAT-09.
